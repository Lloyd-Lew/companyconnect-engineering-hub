First-party implementation note · checked 13 October 2026

An enquiry is complete only after the CRM confirms it.

This note describes CompanyConnect's own website and assistant, not a customer deployment or testimonial. The checks used clearly labelled synthetic contact details in the real published interface and real CRM. Verification date: 13 October 2026.

The constraint

A conversational enquiry could be prepared for review while its confirmation controls extended beyond the usable mobile panel. Separately, opening an assistant or preparing a draft did not establish that an enquiry had reached the CRM. The repair needed to make the final action accessible and distinguish a verified save from intent alone.

The implementation boundary

The enquiry review keeps the confirmation button within the usable panel and makes longer details scrollable. The visitor reviews the contact details before explicitly submitting. The server validates the request, writes the CRM record and checks the saved result before returning a receipt. A completion message is emitted only for a confirmed, nonduplicate save.

  1. Prepare: collect the workflow context and proposed enquiry without treating a draft as a saved lead.
  2. Review: show the exact details and require an explicit confirmation or consent action.
  3. Confirm persistence: use the returned CRM result and readback, not the presence of a button click, as completion evidence.
  4. Measure without contact data: pass a bounded success signal through the existing tag-management bridge. Check the sender and suppress a repeated receipt; keep names, emails, phone numbers and free-text enquiry content out of that measurement message.
  5. Keep tests separate: emit a QA event for reserved synthetic identities, then protect and archive the test records rather than count them as sales opportunities.

Contact-page journey

The tester completed the workflow diagnostic, continued into the reviewed enquiry form and checked consent. Without consent, no submission occurred. One deliberate submission then produced one provider-confirmed CRM record. Independent readback checked the contact details, requested service, source, consent record and staff assignment.

Mobile typed conversation

A real typed assistant session at a 390 × 844 viewport prepared the details for review. No CRM write occurred before confirmation. The Send control was accessible. One deliberate confirmation produced one independently verified CRM record with the intended details and assignment.

Test and measurement hygiene

Each journey emitted one separate QA completion event, not a genuine lead-conversion event. The two test records were protected from follow-up and archived. Exact identity lookups found one matching record per test. No customer records were used for the examples.

What this evidence does not prove

These are two bounded successful journeys, not a throughput benchmark or a guarantee that every future request will succeed. A spoken microphone conversation was not tested. Google Analytics report ingestion remained unverified because the reporting-destination decision was deferred. The note does not claim improved rankings, organic attribution, additional customers, revenue or return on investment.

CompanyConnect retains the dated deployment, UI, server-response and independent CRM readback receipts privately. They are not included here because they contain internal references. This public account deliberately excludes customer data, contact details, administrative links and raw system exports. It is a first-party report, not independent certification.

Architecture translation

From commercial need to technical structure.

A system is not a platform configuration. It is a set of owned, observable, recoverable decisions across people, process, data, integrations, and exceptions.

Business systems

CRM ownership, lead handling, quote and approval, onboarding, operations, invoicing, support, and reporting patterns.

Integration engineering

Validation, transformations, ports/adapters, retry boundaries, idempotency, audit events, and safe failure behavior.

AI-enabled operations

Bounded input, explicit human escalation, data minimization, deterministic fallbacks, and production-readiness requirements.
